## Where to look

### Primary channels (highest signal)

1. **Fiverr Pro** — search "synthwave composer" or "video game soundtrack"
   - Filter: Pro status, 5+ reviews, $500+ average order
   - Expected cost: $200-400/track
   - Pros: fast turnaround, portfolio visible
   - Cons: variable quality, need to vet

2. **SoundBetter** — curated musician marketplace
   - Filter: Electronic / Synthwave / Game Audio
   - Expected cost: $300-600/track
   - Pros: higher-tier talent, direct communication
   - Cons: more expensive

3. **Reddit r/WeAreTheMusicMakers** — post a "hiring" thread
   - Title: "[Hiring] Synthwave composer for mobile game (paid, $2-5K budget)"
   - Pros: direct artist contact, passionate community
   - Cons: lots of unqualified responses, need strong filter

4. **Reddit r/gameai** or r/gamedev** — composer-specific posts
   - Look for "showreel" posts, DM composers whose style fits

5. **Twitter/X #GameAudio** — search hashtag, follow composers
   - Many indie composers post samples regularly
   - DM those whose work fits the synthwave brief

### Secondary channels (worth checking)

6. **Bandcamp** — search synthwave tag, find artists with "for hire" pages
7. **Unity Asset Store** — pre-made synthwave packs (cheap but generic)
8. **Epidemic Sound / Artlist** — licensed library (fallback if composer search fails)

## Evaluation rubric

Score each candidate 1-5 on each axis. Minimum total to advance: 18/25.

| Axis | 1 (poor) | 3 (acceptable) | 5 (excellent) |
|------|----------|----------------|---------------|
| **Style fit** | Doesn't match synthwave brief | Generally fits, some tracks off | All samples match the reference artists |
| **Quality** | Amateur production, weak mixing | Solid production, no major issues | Professional polish, layered stems available |
| **Communication** | Slow responses, vague answers | Replies within 48h, clear answers | Replies within 24h, asks thoughtful questions |
| **Timeline** | Can't meet week 8 first-track deadline | Can deliver 1-2 tracks by week 8 | Full delivery by week 11 |
| **Budget fit** | Wants $10K+ | Wants $4-6K (negotiable) | Wants $2-4K, fits budget |

### Red flags (auto-reject)

- No portfolio or samples shared
- Asks for 100% upfront payment
- Cannot commit to a delivery date
- Demands publishing/royalty rights (we need full buyout)
- Samples are stock loops from sample packs (check with Shazam)

### Green flags

- Has shipped at least 1 game soundtrack before
- Sends a custom 10-15 second demo based on our brief
- Offers stem delivery (separate instrument tracks for future mixing)
- Willing to do 1-2 revision rounds within the price
- Communicates in clear English (or your preferred language)
